Congratulations to Abigail Gyamfi, a member of the Hratchian Group on being recognized by [University of California, Merced, Graduate Division](https://graduatedivision.ucmerced.edu/current-students/awards), with an Outstanding Teaching Award (2022/2023 academic year). The Outstanding Teaching Award (OTA) is intended to recognize the valuable contributions of graduate students to teaching and pedagogy. This award is intended to recognize a body of teaching and pedagogical contributions during the course of graduate education.
