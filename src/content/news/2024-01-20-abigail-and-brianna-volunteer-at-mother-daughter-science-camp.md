Abigail and Brianna volunteered at a Mother-Daughter Science Camp at UC Merced. The camp was sponsored by the <a href="https://ccbm.ucmerced.edu">Center for Cellular and Biomolecular Machines</a>. The camp is a science, technology, engineering and mathematics (STEM) outreach program that brings fourth- and fifth-grade girls and their mothers or other significant women in their lives together to conduct stimulating hands-on experiments. The program consists of sessions on biology, chemistry, math, physics, archaeology, soil science, engineering and computer science. Brianna and Abby did a great job representing the lab and campus as volunteers at this impactful program.
