Congratulations to Abigail on being selected as one of the 2026 [CAS Future Leaders TOP 100 program](https://credentials.cas.org/1b6e1f00-fd40-4a0c-9192-5afc5401dc0a#acc.oy2Srtfi). This is a prestigious award from the CAS Division, American Chemical Society, recognizing the top ~10% of an exceptional PhD and postdoc applicants globally. 

Exclusive benefits include: 1-year free ACS membership, Membership in the CAS Future Leaders Community group and opportunities to attend exclusive virtual training/networking events.
