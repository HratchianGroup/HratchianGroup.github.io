Hrant has been awarded a <a href="https://www.nsf.gov/awardsearch/showAward?AWD_ID=1848580&HistoricalAwards=false">National Science Foundation CARRER Award</a>! This award supports ongoing work in the group to develop and implement new models that address electronic structure posing challenges for current approaches and to explore metal oxide and metal sulfide chemistry using these new models. This award also provides support for undergraduate and graduate education efforts as well exciting new outreach work with local schools. [The UC Merced news story about this award is <a href="https://friendscircle.ucmerced.edu/news/2019/computational-chemist-improving-modeling-complex-processes">online here</a>.]
