Congratulations to Abigail on being recognized with the Merck Next Gen Leaders in Chemistry Award! Abigail was selected among the 16 Winners of the 2025 Merck Next Gen Leaders in Chemistry Award by Merck (https://lnkd.in/p/gSGuwJud) , a research-intensive biopharmaceutical company. Merck recognizes the passion and potential of rising stars in chemical sciences and celebrates the incredible STEM talent that is driving innovation for the future. This competitive national recognition for graduate students and post-doctoral fellows across the USA, is designed to offer mentorship, a series of seminars, and professional development workshops to prepare early-career scientists to succeed in industry and academia.
