Congratulations to Abigail on being recognized with the 2025 ACS Leader Scholars Award! The ACS Leader Scholars program recognizes outstanding undergraduate students, graduate students and early-career scientists. This award provides scholars with year-long access to monthly professional development programs, leadership training, funding and networking opportunities led by professionals across STEM and social sciences in academia and industry.
